# **Getting started**

Welcome to sig icpc, we're happy to see you here!

If it's your first time coming to one of our meetings, it may seem a little confusing on how to get started. Luckily, there most likely will be a couple of officiers in this meeting to help you out, but in the meantime you can also follow these steps! 


## Create a [Codeforces](https://codeforces.com/) account

[Codeforces](https://codeforces.com/) is the largest platform for competitive programming, and a majority of our meetings will be on it. 

![register](/assets/images/start.jpg)


## Join the [UIUC Codeforces Group]((https://codeforces.com/group/tKC7z9Nm0A))

This is where all our Codeforces practices will take place in. 

[Our Codeforces Group Link](https://codeforces.com/group/tKC7z9Nm0A)

## Join the current meeting

Click on **contests** 
![codeforces contest](/assets/images/start1.jpg)


Here you will see a list of contests (this list displays past contests/meetings as well).

From here, click on the **Register** button -> register as individual -> **Enter** to enter the contest.
